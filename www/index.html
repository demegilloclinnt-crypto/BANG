MO₂ Monitoring System

How to use: fill in steps 1 → 6 from top to bottom. Everything saves automatically on this computer. Step 7 shows your monthly trend.

🔄 A newer version of this app is available.

1Date & cylinder stock

Pick the date (you can open past days too) and count the cylinders in storage.

Opening cylinders are filled in from yesterday’s closing balance — change them if your physical count differs. The heat index is entered for 5 days in step 6.

2Patients on oxygen, by flow rate (LPM)

Type how many patients are on each flow rate, per station. Leave blank if none. Each LPM uses LPM × 1,440 liters per patient per day.

1 LPM (low)10 LPM (high)

3Result: is our oxygen enough?

Calculated automatically for the next 24 hours. Stock risk = cylinders needed in 24 hours ÷ full cylinders on hand × 100. Below 50% safe · 50–75% watch · 75–100% warning · 100% or more critical. The heat index (°C) is the “feels-like” temperature from step 1.

4Tanks released to each station

Choose the station, enter how many tanks left storage, then press Release. Mistake? Press “undo” beside the entry.

5Deliveries received & cylinder balance

When the supplier delivers, enter the full cylinders received and any empties handed back. The balance updates automatically: opening + received − released = closing.

65-day heat index & O₂ per patient

Type the heat index (°C) for each of the 5 days. Next to it you see the oxygen used per patient that day, so you can compare oxygen use with the heat. Click a date to open that day.

7Monthly monitoring & trend

Every day you fill in is saved here automatically. Click a point on the chart or a row in the table to open that day.

● = day has saved data

Heat index is the “feels-like” temperature in °C, entered in the 5-day table (step 6) (category bands follow PAGASA). Each dot is one day — click it to open that day.

Press Submit (top right) and the data of the day on screen is emailed to everyone below. Anyone using the app — on the PC or any phone — can press it.
